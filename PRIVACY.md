# Music Remover privacy policy

Music Remover removes background music from the audio of videos and web pages. It does all of its work on your computer.

**Audio.** To remove the music, the extension reads the audio of the videos you play on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn, and of any tab where you turn on live mode. This audio is processed inside your browser and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

**Settings.** Your choices (on or off, how much music to remove, model quality, live mode delay and language) are saved with Chrome's extension storage, which Chrome may sync between your devices if you use Chrome sync.

**Model download.** The first time it is needed, the extension downloads its AI model from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) and keeps it in the browser's cache. This request contains no information about you or what you watch.

**What we do not do.** Music Remover has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. It does not collect, sell or share any personal data or browsing activity.

**Contact.** Questions about this policy can go to the developer contact shown on the extension's Chrome Web Store page.
